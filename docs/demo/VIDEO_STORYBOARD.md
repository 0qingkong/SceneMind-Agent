## Editing guidance

- Record a clean browser viewport without developer tools, notifications, personal bookmarks, local paths, or unrelated tabs.
- Use 1920×1080 or 1440×900 capture and preserve the app's Demo/Mock/Simulator labels.
- Keep cuts calm enough for readable Chinese UI; avoid speed ramps over claims or limitations.
- Do not crop the global Profile C banner, analyzer engine, 2D disclaimer, or glasses disclaimer.
- Use only approved screenshots from `presentation/screenshots/`; staging screenshots are not release assets.
- Final recording, voiceover, subtitle appearance, privacy review, and end-to-end playback require human approval.
